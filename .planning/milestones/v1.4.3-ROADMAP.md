## Overview

This milestone delivered a configurable token launch experience for OpenLaunch. Starting from a working launchpad with hardcoded defaults, the work progressively exposed all launch parameters through a guided form, handled complex parameter combinations safely on-chain, and ensured the full lifecycle (creation → monitoring → fee tracking) respects user choices. All 29 v1 requirements were satisfied and verified through UAT/verification reports.
